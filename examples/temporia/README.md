# Example: Temporia

A real project used to show how the pack is configured. **Everything in this folder is an example, not part of the pack.** Delete the folder in your fork.

**The project:** Temporia is a quiz web app, "the Duolingo of history and culture", to rediscover your region and practice slow tourism. Social media (Instagram, TikTok) is its main channel before launch.

**What the profile shows**
- A *narrow scope*: history facts, heritage, regional food and know-how, and the app itself. `studio-ideas` will never suggest anything else.
- A *strict fact policy*: statistics, partnerships and promised rewards are listed in `never_invent`, so the pack asks instead of making things up.
- *Voice rules* that matter in practice: informal address, one sign-off line, one call to action, placed at the end.
- *Video enabled*: scripts from `studio-create` are rendered by `studio-video`.
- *Notion* as tracker, *Metricool* as stats source, comparisons only on groups of 5 posts or more.

**Try it:** copy `profile.yml` to `studio/profile.yml` in a scratch folder, then ask Claude: "give me 5 ideas for next week" and watch it stay inside the four domains.

See `sample-content-log.csv` for what logged records look like (illustrative rows, no real facts) and `sample-video-spec.json` for a scene spec.
